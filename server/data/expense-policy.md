# Acme Global Corporate Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document Reference:** POL-FIN-2026-V1  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Scope:** All Employees, Contractors, and Consultants  

---

## Section 1: General Expense Requirements
### 1.1 Business Purpose and Eligibility
All expenses submitted for reimbursement must be directly incurred in the ordinary course of performing official business duties for Acme Global. Personal, recreational, or unauthorized purchases are strictly non-reimbursable. Employees must exercise prudent financial judgment and treat company resources as they would their own.

### 1.2 Accuracy and Substantiation
Every expense claim must include a clear, descriptive justification specifying the business purpose, client/project context, and participants where applicable. Providing misleading, incomplete, or false descriptions constitutes a policy violation and may result in disciplinary action.

### 1.3 Currency and Foreign Expenses
Claims are primarily denominated in Indian Rupees (INR). Claims incurred in foreign currencies (USD, EUR, GBP) must reflect actual conversion rates or exchange vouchers. Spending limits specified in this policy apply directly to INR claims; foreign currency conversions are reviewed under equivalent threshold evaluations.

---

## Section 2: Travel Expenses
### 2.1 Authorized Travel and Spending Limit
Business travel includes transportation expenses incurred while traveling to client locations, branch offices, or business-critical offsites. The maximum reimbursable threshold for standard travel expenses is ₹10,000 per claim or trip leg.

### 2.2 Reimbursable Travel Modes
Reimbursable travel expenses include domestic economy airfare, standard rail fares (up to AC 2-Tier), pre-booked intercity cabs, app-based intracity taxis (e.g., Uber, Ola), airport transfers, and authorized toll or parking fees. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed per standard company rate upon odometer log submission.

### 2.3 Non-Reimbursable Travel
Premium economy upgrades, business class airfare (unless approved in writing by VP), flight cancellation penalties caused by personal delays, flight insurance, personal vehicle maintenance, and traffic or parking violation fines are non-reimbursable.

---

## Section 3: Meals & Entertainment
### 3.1 Policy Limit and Meal Guidelines
Business meals with clients, partners, prospective candidates, or project team working sessions are eligible for reimbursement. The maximum reimbursable limit for Meals & Entertainment is ₹2,000 per claim.

### 3.2 Working Meals and Team Dinners
Working lunches or dinners require advance managerial notification when exceeding 3 internal participants. All attendees (internal employees and external guests) and the specific business discussion agenda must be documented in the claim description.

### 3.3 Alcohol and Extravagant Dining
Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable unless specifically pre-approved in writing for an authorized corporate banquet or client entertainment event. Lavish, extravagant, or nightclub expenses are strictly prohibited under company policy.

---

## Section 4: Equipment
### 4.1 Equipment Purchase Limit
To support remote and in-office work, employees may procure authorized peripheral hardware, monitors, keyboards, noise-canceling headsets, webcams, ergonomic chairs, and laptop accessories. The maximum reimbursable limit for Equipment is ₹15,000 per claim.

### 4.2 Asset Ownership and IT Approval
All equipment purchased through corporate reimbursement remains company property. Purchases of computing devices, tablets, smartphones, and servers require prior approval from IT Infrastructure and Procurement. Duplicate hardware purchases within a 24-month period are not permissible.

---

## Section 5: Office Supplies
### 5.1 Eligible Supplies and Spending Limit
Expenditures for routine consumable office supplies necessary for official duties are reimbursable up to a maximum limit of ₹5,000 per claim.

### 5.2 Covered Items
Eligible supplies include printer paper, ink/toner cartridges, notebooks, pens, mailing envelopes, sticky notes, whiteboard accessories, and standard desk organization supplies. Claims for personal home furnishings, non-business stationery, or decorative items will be rejected.

---

## Section 6: Training & Education
### 6.1 Professional Development Limit
Acme Global encourages continuous professional skill development. Reimbursement for job-relevant training courses, technical certifications, professional conference passes, workshops, and educational materials is allowed up to ₹10,000 per claim.

### 6.2 Pre-requisites and Proof of Completion
Training programs must directly relate to the employee's current role or planned organizational progression. Reimbursement requires manager endorsement and submission of formal course registration, invoice, and certificate of successful completion or attendance.

---

## Section 7: Medical Expenses
### 7.1 Occupational and Health Reimbursement Limit
Reimbursement for authorized occupational health checks, corporate wellness programs, required travel vaccinations, ergonomic health assessments, and emergency first-aid supplies is supported up to ₹5,000 per claim.

### 7.2 Non-Covered Medical Costs
Routine prescription medications covered under company medical insurance, elective cosmetic treatments, and non-prescribed dietary supplements are not eligible for expense claim reimbursement and should be processed through the health insurance administrator.

---

## Section 8: Receipt Requirements
### 8.1 Mandatory Itemized Receipts
An itemized receipt, tax invoice, or official payment bill is strictly mandatory for every expense claim where the claimed amount is greater than ₹0.

### 8.2 Receipt Validity Criteria
Valid receipts must explicitly state: (a) merchant/vendor name, (b) date of transaction, (c) itemized breakdown of goods or services purchased, (d) total amount and currency, and (e) proof of payment completion. Credit card summary slips that do not display itemized breakdowns are insufficient.

### 8.3 Missing Receipt Protocol
Claims submitted with missing receipts (receiptAvailable marked as false) are flagged for review and cannot be processed for payment without an official Missing Receipt Declaration and manager sign-off.

---

## Section 9: Duplicate Claims
### 9.1 Prohibition of Duplicate Submissions
Submitting duplicate claims for the same expense—whether intentionally or accidentally—is strictly prohibited under corporate finance governance.

### 9.2 Duplicate Detection Criteria
An expense is classified as a suspected duplicate if another claim exists sharing the same claimant, transaction date, expense category, amount, and currency. Automated validation will immediately place suspected duplicate claims into UNDER_REVIEW status. Repeated duplicate submissions are subjected to an internal audit review.

---

## Section 10: Submission Dates & Date Rules
### 10.1 Timely Submission Window
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the transaction date. Claims submitted after 60 days require Department Head approval and justification.

### 10.2 Future Dates Prohibited
The date of the expense claim must represent an actual past or present transaction date. Claims bearing future dates are considered invalid, violate accounting audit standards, and are flagged as DATE_FUTURE errors.

---

## Section 11: Exceptions and Manager Approval
### 11.1 General Category ("Other") Limit
Miscellaneous authorized business expenses not covered under specific category buckets are classified as "Other" and are subject to a maximum spending limit of ₹5,000 per claim.

### 11.2 Over-Limit Policy Exceptions
Any expense exceeding policy limits or deviating from standard guidelines requires prior written authorization from the Department Head and Finance Controller. Claims without prior authorization will be adjusted down to the maximum allowable limit or placed under review.
